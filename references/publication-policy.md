# Publication and editorial operations

Daily plan: 1 China-focused album and 1 international album, both manually chosen. Editorial calendar is aspirational; quality/safety beats volume.

State machine: SELECTED -> RESEARCHED -> WRITTEN -> CHECKED -> HUMAN_APPROVED -> DRAFT_SAVED -> PUBLISHED -> MEASURED.

Publisher integrations must be modular and disabled by default. Research permissions are separate from publishing permissions. NEVER treat “run the daily workflow” as authorization to publish without review. Require an explicit per-post current-session confirmation and a supported official or otherwise permitted posting channel. For WorkBuddy, confirm real Skill/MCP capabilities before claiming compatibility.

Use original album artwork only under appropriate rights/use permission. Do not upload full music files. Do not put personal essays, credentials or private draft archives in a public repo.

Weekly metrics: successful researched albums, sources per album and source diversity, editorial correction count, time spent, editorial approval rate, notes' engagement and account health; do not fabricate outcomes or purchase engagement.
