# Story craft & Editorial QA v0.2

## Narrative architecture (not a rigid template)
A. Small concrete contradiction or observed fact; no invented life anecdotes.
B. Establish the record's artistic situation and the stakes of making it.
C. Return to at least three sonic examples as proof, not a biography dump.
D. Introduce a contrary reading, weakness or limit; do not over-praise.
E. Close with a changed understanding or an unresolved honest question.

## Diagnostics
- Underline every sentence about the artist's life: can it be sourced? Does it earn its place in an album review?
- Highlight every sonic statement: what track, what instrument/phrasing/rhythm/production and which evidence?
- Delete any general phrase that fits almost any album, especially easy 'loss and growth' tropes.
- No writer's fake first-person listening, concerts, relationships, or memories.
- Contrast source-derived narrative and your own claim; ensure independently reasoned argument.
- Counterclaim should be meaningful, not a token 'some tracks are average'.
- Run sentence overlap screening AND manual structure/argument comparison against sources; a numeric similarity score cannot certify originality.
- When album music is inadequately available, ask editor to listen and record three notes rather than hallucinatory close listening.

## Content package QA
research source inventory; sonic evidence matrix; reviewed narrative thesis; album cover rights; headline/body; carousel cards; final human approval. Social posting disabled unless explicit approval.
