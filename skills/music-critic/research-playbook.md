# Deep Research Playbook v0.2

## Search budget and stopping
Do 4–6 distinct research passes, not a single search result page. Use Chinese, English, original-language artist spelling, alternate release titles, album year, song titles, and site-targeted searches. Minimum target (not guaranteed): 12 unique relevant URLs from 6 independently operated publishers/platforms; at least 2 primary sources or a visible primary-source gap; at least 3 professional critical sources when available; 2 listener-community perspectives; 1 contemporaneous source when available. This is a search effort threshold, NOT permission to invent sources; if unmet, mark RESEARCH_INCOMPLETE with precise gaps. Stop when 2 consecutive passes deliver no material new evidence, not when arbitrary hit count is reached.

Pass A: credits, release variants, catalog number, producers, sessions, studio musicians, lyrics/authors, tracklist (official/label/liner).
Pass B: career arc, previous release, local scene, decisions, economics, contemporaneous artist interviews.
Pass C: targeted music journals and magazines, historic press and local-language reporting.
Pass D: RYM, Douban, Discogs, AOTY, fan communities, verified Weibo sources; capture disagreements without overgeneralization.
Pass E: each key song + production/instruments/vocals/lyrics/structure; separate direct audio listening by human from descriptions in press.
Pass F: follow source references, locate original quotes and challenge strongest interpretations.

## Specific source queries (illustrative)
artist album interview making-of production 1990/2018; artist album review Pitchfork / Paste / AllMusic / Bandcamp Daily / local press; album site:rateyourmusic.com; artist song lyrics production credits; album site:weibo.com; original-language interview.
Keep per-source access status SUCCESS/NOT_FOUND/LOGIN_REQUIRED/RATE_LIMITED/UNAVAILABLE and why. Do not circumvent login restrictions.

## Research output minimum
- Discography & production timeline, with at least three dated events where evidence permits.
- Distinct primary/interview, professional critic, listener-community, and current reference sections.
- Sonic Evidence Matrix: track, sonic claim, musical dimension, evidence, whether FIRSTHAND_EDITOR / REPORTED_BY_CRITIC / CREDIT_BASED / HYPOTHESIS.
- Reception disagreement: source A says X, B says Y; what each hears differently.
- At least 2 original editorial theses plus counterarguments; cite individual fact IDs.
- Missing-evidence report and precise questions for human listening.
