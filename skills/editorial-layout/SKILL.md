# Xiaohongshu Layout v0.2

Default: use the legally authorized original album cover AS THE FIRST IMAGE without replacing it with generated artwork, text stickers, cropping out critical design, or AI repainting. Retain sources and rights notes. Subsequent cards are restrained editorial typography: optional 3–5 clean text slides produced from the same review; no extra images necessary.

## Suggested carousel
1. Original album cover (authorized). No extra graphic text by default.
2. Listening thesis / specific music note (large readable headline, 60–110 Chinese characters).
3. Sonic close reading with track name, arrangement and a source/verification badge.
4. People/place/recording chronology (factual and attributed).
5. Tension or counterargument + 3 recommended tracks.
Only use cards for which adequate verified material exists. An album-only cover post is acceptable; default design is minimal, not mandatory five cards.

## Typography & accessibility
1080 × 1440 output per optional slide (3:4), 64–100 px title, 37–48 px body, safe outer margin >= 84 px. Limit blocks to avoid overflows; no tiny footnotes as fake citations. Solid high contrast. HTML rendered as images by browser or Playwright where permitted. Keep HTML source and outputs for revision.

## Content/rights
Do not infer permission from public availability of album art. The album cover can be linked rather than rehosted until rights/use clearance. Never download promotional photos without permission. No watermark removal. No music file uploads.

## Quality gates
visual line breaks and overflow, Chinese punctuation, proper names, no unsupported quotes, cover aspect ratio, no hard-to-read decorative noise. Human preview mandatory before posting.
