## Material discovery
- Online service metadata can conflict with reference compilation dates; distinguish original pressing, digital version, reissue.
- A label/artist page can resolve recording details that Wikipedia does not.
- MusicBrainz lists variants; never assume a single track count applies to all editions.
- RYM inaccessible != no reviews. Record access and seek legally accessible community alternatives.
